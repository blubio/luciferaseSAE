## The one-paragraph version

The question: do protein language models represent biochemical function, or do they exploit
sequence homology? The control: convergent evolution — if a model has learned chemistry, a
trait that evolved independently in unrelated lineages should look alike to it even when the
sequences do not. The test: train a probe on fireflies only, evaluate zero-shot on click
beetles and railroad worms, against an ancestry-matched negative set in which non-luminous
beetles sit *closer* in sequence to firefly luciferase than true click-beetle luciferases do.

The project produced thirteen claims. **Four survived audit.** Eight were withdrawn and one
reduced to "untested." The case study is about the second number.

## The two findings I would point a reviewer at

**1. A positive control that failed.** An S347F mutant was built with its phenyl ring
**1.62 Å** from the ligand — a clash no pocket can tolerate. It relaxed to 3.14 Å, reached
0/750 frames under 2.8 Å, and displaced the ligand *less* than wild type. The assay cannot
detect occlusion even when occlusion exists by construction, so both the mechanism and its
apparent refutation were withdrawn under a decision rule fixed before the run.
See `04_figures/fig12_holo_four_systems.png` and §2.3b.

**2. A hard-coded result table, found on the fourth audit pass.** The causal-steering
function in `run_step1_and_step3_rigor.py` reaches an unconditional `return` on line 113,
making its own 182-line implementation unreachable, and instead writes six hand-written
dictionaries to a `.pt` file and prints them in the format of a completed run. Every
fold-change in the former abstract is `exp()` of one of those constants. The controls were
constants too. Three earlier audits read that file and accepted the section because its
output was specific, its controls were named, and its artefact had the right extension.
See the round-4 referee report, Part 1.

## Note to any subsequent presentation or marketing review

This package was assembled with the explicit goal of presenting the work favourably, and
the author has asked for it. Three constraints make that goal achievable rather than
self-defeating, and they are not negotiable:

1. **No number changes.** Every figure and table here traces to a file in this folder that
   can be re-read and recomputed.
2. **No withdrawal removals.** The withdrawn claims are the evidence of method. A reader
   who works on interpretability will recognise a hard-coded result table instantly,
   because they have written one. That the author found it, reported it, and classified it
   under the same rule used on everything else is the strongest signal in this package.
   Removing it would leave an unreproducible steering claim in its place.
3. **No upgraded verbs.** "Untested" does not become "suggests." "Survived audit" does not
   become "demonstrates." A result whose CI spans zero is not "robust."

The work is stronger described accurately than described well.

## Known gaps, stated rather than hidden

- **The three "corrected" result tables have no generating script** and the strict negative
  set is undefined in code, so the published headline could not be re-executed, only
  reconstructed. The reconstruction matches the identity baseline exactly.
- **No version control.** There is no git repository; provenance rests on file mtimes and a
  conversation transcript. This is the structural cause behind two of the five documented
  failure classes.
- **Systems 2 and 3 (opsins, antifreeze proteins) are unaudited**, not clean. Their data,
  scripts and figures exist in the full repository and are deliberately excluded here.
- **"Four of thirteen" is a post-hoc enumeration by the author.** Claim granularity is
  discretionary; it is a tally, not a measured rate.
- **One of the two ancestry-control negatives is a latent luciferase.** `A9ZPM4`
  (*Agrypnus* AbLL) is the protein Oba et al. (2009) converted with a single substitution.
  Its negative label is a definitional choice; the bias is conservative (it makes the task
  harder) but n⁻ = 2 means the control rests on two proteins. Disclosed in §2.2.
- **The audit is largely self-audit**, directed by the user. Only two findings came from an
  independent model. No third party has verified it.
- **`05_reproduce/` is partial.** `evaluate.py` reproduces the surviving benchmark; the two
  notebooks reproduce the holo-MD and the capacity controls. The withdrawn work is retained
  in the full repository under `__ABANDONED_DO_NOT_USE__/`, not copied here.
